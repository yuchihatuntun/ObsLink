> 2016 年英二真题

### Part A

Thank you for your congratulation on my triumph in the competition. Hearing that you need some advice on translation, I would do my best to summarize my experience for you.

First and foremost, our origin translation tend to be too straight and unfitted. In order to solve this problem, you can read some official journals to accumulate professional words that is usually used in translation. Secondly, the amount of vocabulary that you master also is the key to accurate translation. The more words you know, the better translation can you make.

The experience above is the true key that I keep preparing during the preparation of the contest, I sincerely hope that they would help you with your progression on translation.
